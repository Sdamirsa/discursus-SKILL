# Final Qualities — the discursus scientific-writing rubric

A checkable distillation of what a strong manuscript Discussion must do. Grade each
applicable item **PASS / RISK / FAIL** with the offending text quoted and a concrete fix.

---

## §A. The discussion within the paper

- **A1 Grounded & scoped.** Every move is grounded in the results and scoped to the main
  aim and gap. It *discusses* the intro/methods/results — it does not repeat them.
- **A2 Same spine, different jobs.** Intro, methods, results, discussion sit on one axis
  (the spine) but do different jobs with different tone and architecture. The discussion's
  job is interpretation and consequence, not narration.
- **A3 A genuine conceptual advance.** The contribution reframes understanding, not just
  better numbers on a known task. Increment alone does not clear the bar.
- **A4 Calibrated & defensible.** Claims are scoped precisely; alternative explanations are
  addressed; nothing overreaches. The work would survive a broad editorial table.

## §B. Within the discussion

- **B1 One-sentence claim.** The central claim can be written as a single sentence, and
  every section visibly serves it.
- **B2 Reverse-outline test.** The one-line takeaway of each section/paragraph, read alone
  top to bottom, tells the entire story. Gaps or non-sequiturs here are structural, not
  wording, problems.
- **B3 Gap-fill alignment.** The intro's gap, the results, and the discussion's payoff
  describe the *same* gap. The results fill exactly that gap — no scope creep, no
  unanswered promise.
- **B4 Logical-chain order.** Points are sequenced as statements that build toward the
  claim (the argument's logic), not the chronological order experiments were run.
- **B5 No zig-zag / parallelism.** Only the central idea recurs; every other subject lives
  in exactly one place. Parallel items (datasets, models, experiments) appear in the same
  order each time.
- **B6 Breadth.** A non-specialist editor can see why this matters from the outline alone.
  If the value needs very local context, it is a field-journal paper.

### Opening paragraph
- **B7 Answer first.** Opens by stating the central claim as now established, in plain
  language, then supports it — an interpretation of the key findings, not a replay of
  results.
- **B8 Closes the intro's gap.** Opens exactly where the introduction's gap left off; it
  visibly answers the question the intro promised.

### Interpretation, not restatement
- **B9 Every results reference adds meaning.** No sentence merely re-quotes a number; each
  says what it implies or why it matters.
- **B10 No new data.** No figures, analyses, or numbers appear here that are not already in
  Results.

### Contribution
- **B11 One quotable sentence.** There is a single sentence a reviewer could lift as "what
  this changes." It is stated, not implied.

### Literature dialogue
- **B12 Engages disagreement.** Cites work that reaches different conclusions and explains
  why this study differs — not only confirming work.
- **B13 Not a second introduction.** Context is confined to the most recent and most
  relevant work; it is not a full literature review.

### Unexpected results
- **B14 Engaged, not buried.** Surprising, null, or contradictory findings are addressed
  thoughtfully.

### Claims matched to evidence
- **B15 Strength matches design.** Claim strength matches the study design; what is
  supported now is separated from what is still inference. Observational / single-center /
  narrowly benchmarked work sounds like it knows that.
- **B16 Honest generalizability.** Distribution shift; transfer across sites, scanners,
  populations; comparison against standard of care or existing models; known failure modes
  — named, not assumed away. (Especially for model / world-model work.)

### Limitations
- **B17 Functional limitations.** Short, specific, calm; each says what it *forbids you to
  claim* ("cohort size makes the subgroup analyses exploratory"), not "small sample, more
  research needed."
- **B18 The obvious ones are present**, and alternative explanations for the main result
  are ruled out or openly acknowledged.

### Closing paragraph
- **B19 What changes?** Answers the single question of what changes because of this result
  — a concrete implication or specific next step, not boilerplate "future work."

## §C. Within a paragraph

- **C1 One point.** Nameable in a short phrase. If it needs two phrases, split the
  paragraph.
- **C2 First sentence = context.** Orients the reader and signals why this, now. A reader
  should never ask "why is this here?"
- **C3 Last sentence = takeaway.** States the conclusion to remember — not a stray detail
  or a dangling transition.
- **C4 Internal flow, no zig-zag.** Middle sentences advance the one point in a line; each
  connects to the previous (old information first, new last).
- **C5 Parallelism.** Parallel items use the same structure and order, so differences stand
  out.
- **C6 Support & calibration.** Every claim is backed by a citation, data point, or figure
  pointer, and language strength matches evidence strength.
- **C7 Signal-to-noise.** Every sentence advances the one point; orphaned facts and
  "interesting but irrelevant" asides are deleted.
- **C8 Results paragraphs specifically.** Move through question/approach → result → what
  it establishes, tied explicitly to the figure; the sequence of such paragraphs reads as
  the logical proof of the central claim.
