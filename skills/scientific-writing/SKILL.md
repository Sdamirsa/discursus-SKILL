# Scientific writing rubric (single source of truth)

This skill is the canonical definition of "good" for the discursus system. Reviewer agents
and quality gates grade against it, so "quality" means one thing everywhere — no drift.

The full, checkable rubric is in **`final-qualities.md`**. Read it before grading.

## Three altitudes (see final-qualities.md for the checklists)

- **Whole discussion (§A)** — grounded in results, scoped to the gap, a genuine conceptual
  advance (not an increment), calibrated and defensible at a broad editorial table.
- **Across the discussion (§B)** — one-sentence claim; reverse-outline tells the whole
  story; gap-fill alignment (intro gap = results = payoff); logical chain, not chronology;
  no zig-zag / parallelism; explicit contribution; calibrated literature dialogue; honest
  generalizability; functional limitations; opening answers the gap; closing answers
  "what changes".
- **Within a paragraph (§C)** — one point; first sentence = context; last = takeaway;
  old→new internal flow; parallelism for parallel items; every claim supported and
  calibrated; high signal-to-noise.

## How to grade

For each applicable criterion, return **PASS / RISK / FAIL**, quote the offending text, and
give a specific rewrite or fix. Match language strength to evidence — flag **both**
overclaim and needless hedging. Never invent data, numbers, or sources; if a check needs
material you do not have, say so rather than guessing.
